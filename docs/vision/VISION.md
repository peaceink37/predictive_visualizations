# Product Vision — Project Reality

## Narrative
Project Reality is a predictive visualization platform designed to help people **see reality before it arrives**.

Most analytical tools describe the past.
Project Reality lets users **interrogate the future**.

---

## The Core Problem
Modern decision-makers are drowning in dashboards that:
- Explain yesterday
- Obscure causality
- Collapse under complexity

Critical systems — energy, water, logistics, climate, economics — are **interdependent**, yet modeled in isolation.

---

## The Breakthrough
Project Reality transforms static data into:
- Editable scenarios
- Predictive timelines
- Interactive narratives

Users don’t just view data — they **explore consequences**.

---

## The Finished Product (Future State)

### Capabilities
- Multi-dataset ingestion (public + private)
- Cross-domain simulation layers
- Predictive controls (“mixing board” interfaces)
- Time-horizon navigation (days → decades)
- Explainable deltas, not black-box outputs

### Experience
- Canvas-native, fluid, non-clunky visuals
- Narrative overlays and annotations
- Exportable insights, not raw charts

---

## Customer Segments
- Energy planners and grid operators
- Policy analysts
- Infrastructure investors
- Research institutions
- High-stakes corporate strategists

These users pay for **foresight**, not charts.

---

## Why This Wins
- Dashboards are read-only — Project Reality is exploratory
- Spreadsheets scale poorly — Project Reality thrives on complexity
- Traditional BI avoids prediction — Project Reality embraces it

---

Electricity is the wedge.

From there:

Not as features — as inevitabilities.
