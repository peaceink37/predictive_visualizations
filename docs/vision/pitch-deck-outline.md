# Pitch Deck Outline — Project Reality

## 1. Title
**Project Reality**
Predictive visualization for complex systems

---

## 2. The Problem
- Decision-makers rely on backward-looking dashboards
- Static charts fail under complexity
- Critical systems are becoming more volatile and interconnected

---

## 3. Why Now
- Energy transition pressure
- AI-driven demand growth (data centers)
- Regulatory and infrastructure uncertainty
- Abundance of data, scarcity of insight

---

## 4. Existing Solutions Fall Short
- BI tools are read-only
- Spreadsheets don’t scale
- Simulation tools are inaccessible or opaque

---

## 5. The Breakthrough
Project Reality turns data into:
- Editable scenarios
- Predictive timelines
- Visual cause-and-effect

Users explore outcomes before they commit.

---

## 6. The Product (Future State)
- Multi-dataset modeling
- Predictive controls (“mixing board”)
- Time-horizon navigation
- Narrative overlays
- Canvas-native performance

---

## 7. The Wedge (MVP)
- Electricity generation visualization
- One dataset, one control
- Proof that prediction + interaction works

---

## 8. Market
- Energy planners
- Policy analysts
- Infrastructure investors
- Research institutions

High-stakes users with high willingness to pay.

---

## 9. Expansion
Electricity → Water → Transport → Climate → Economics

A platform, not a point solution.

---

## 10. Why Us
- Systems thinking
- Engineering rigor
- Narrative clarity

Rare combination.

---

## 11. The Ask
- Capital to scale product and team
- Time to turn inevitability into infrastructure
